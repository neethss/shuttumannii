Ennik ariyaam idh cringe ahn enn, but still idhokke oru fun alle.

Maybe njyan kazhinj adutha oru pennine nee nokkumbol aayirikkum idhokke manasilaavuka. Endhayaalum, orikkal manasilaavum.

Endhokke ahnelum ennikk ninne othiri ishtava da. Ingane ulla ishtam ennikk aarodum thoneet illa, ini thonuvem illa.

Ennikk ninne venm enn athrekyum vaashi ahn. Aa vaashi karanam, ninne ennikk eppolum kittatte enn aagrahikkunnu.
